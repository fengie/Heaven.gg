It does not by itself establish the exact client's edge rules, individual costs, or version compatibility. Its source claims are not a substitute for official mechanics or a direct in-game demonstration. No numeric weights were copied into the graph from it.

Official-site searches found count data and update announcements but no complete verifiable per-node acquisition/adjacency specification in the retrieved pages. Therefore adjacency and weights remain unverified, not presumed absent from the game. A future attempt should target actual tooltips/client-exported rules rather than repeat these same searches.
