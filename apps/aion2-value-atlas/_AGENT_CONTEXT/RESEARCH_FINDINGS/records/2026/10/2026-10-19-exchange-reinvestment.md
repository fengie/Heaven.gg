# AION 2 Global — sustainable Kina → gear → Kina → Quna strategy

Verified/researched on **2026-10-19** (America/New_York). Owning product: `fengie/Heaven.gg` / Atreia Atlas. Scope: Global Season 1, not KR/TW live rules. No automatic trading, purchases, market scraping or character/account data. This updates, rather than duplicates, the previous October 19 exchange observation. ## Executive decision

**First maximize net tradeable Kina per Odyle claim and per active hour; reserve bound Kina for upgrades and Odyle costs; promote surplus tradeable Kina to Quna only after the next profitable gear gate and operating buffer are financed.** For a Spiritmaster ("Summoner") archetype, maximize reliable orb/spirit PvE clears at the cheapest viable item-level breakpoint; do not buy speculative +15 upgrades or alts before the main's route is measured. Evidence for a single class build is editorial, not a personalized measured DPS benchmark.

- **Buying Kina using Quna:** at these asks, buy 2M denomination when available. Exactly 10M Kina costs 5 × 950 = **4,750 Quna**, compared with 5,425 in 400K chests or 5,699 in the large chest: save **949 Quna** versus one 10M. Depth at the minimum quote is not established by `Sales List`.
- **Selling earned Kina for Quna:** the large chest has the best *nominal* price per Kina. However, selling 10M at 5,699 Quna yields only **246.6 more net Quna** than 25 small chests sold at 217 with the same total morph Kina (11M): 5,129.1 vs 4,882.5. It must have sufficient actual sell-through and shorter/cost-effective holding time. Price threshold: prefer 10M **only above 25 × the 400K clearing price** (5,425 at this snapshot), with confirmed liquidity and fees. Otherwise stagger smaller listings and measure actual Settlement Details.
- **No chest-round-trip arbitrage:** 5 × 2M bought for 4,750 Quna provide 10M; morphing a new large chest still needs another 1M Kina, worth approximately 475 Quna at the same buyer exchange rate. Total input ~5,225 Quna exceeds the large chest's ~5,129.1 net receipt. The nominal 5,699 ask hides a loss.
- Treat tradeable Kina as investment capital until the next profitable upgrade is funded. Bound Kina cannot be used to morph chests or purchase Market items.

### Opportunity cost of gathered Odyle — often overlooked

Gathering materials from Lower Reshanta compete with morphing them for dungeon claims. The recipe for +40 Odyle Energy uses **25 Odyle, 5 Fine Odyle, 1 Pure Odyle, and 50K Kina**. One 16 October server-specific 60-minute manual farm sold Odyle materials at roughly **362K Kina net per one morph recipe**, accounting for a reported 2% Market listing fee and 10% sale tax; this is **one vendor's dated sample**, not today's universal price: https://raidrelay.com/aion-2-odyle-farming-guide .

At that observed resale rate, morphing gathered materials only beats selling the materials for currency (without valuing gear/other drops or dungeon time) when the additional cube reward exceeds **362K + 50K = ~412K Kina**. Thus 300K Conquest 2★ and 400K Conquest 3★ lose to selling ingredients on that server. Transcendence 4's 480K has a narrow 68K advantage *before* additional time and consumables, so selling can still be better. For members, buying 40 energy for 100K from Wind Breeze Merchants avoids consuming gathering materials. For non-members without Market access, morphing can still convert bound Kina and gathered materials into tradeable cube rewards. Check actual item tradeability, trust level, listing slots, regional rules and sale receipts: https://lum.gg/aion-2/guides/market-guide . Site claims on free-account selling conflict; conservative assumption is **no buy or sell without Special Quai Membership** until the actual client proves otherwise.

One October 16 one-hour manual Lower Reshanta route reportedly generated **~2.82M Kina net of listing fees** (including one lucky Radiant Odyle); excluding the rare Radiant, ~2.13M. These are **sample asks** and not guaranteed executed sales; server competition, player danger, account trust flags and human gathering pace may materially lower realized returns. Do not rely on bots/macros or third-party account pilots: NCSOFT explicitly prohibits illicit currency acquisition. Source: https://lounge.plaync.com/feed/82972?country=US&locale=en-US .

## Character reinvestment decisions: payback, not vanity item level

**Baseline rule:** For every feasible upgrade or farming alternative, compare `(expected tradeable net reward + realized sellable loot - variable costs) / (queue + run + recovery minutes)`, plus the separate weekly payout ceiling and scarcity of Odyle. Use actual clear-time medians (at least three clears) and actual Market completed settlements rather than asking prices. Investment `C` only pays back after `ceil(C / (incremental net Kina per claim))` claims. If the new tier adds queues, consumables, failures or a weekly cap, include them.

- `1,400 → 2,100` improves listed reward **100K/claim**. With 21 natural claims, that's +2.1M/week; with all 41 temporary member claims, **+4.1M/week** before added time/cost. A hypothetical **4M Kina** gating upgrade recovers in ~40 equal-rate claims, around a week *only if* those 41 claims remain eligible and efficient. Compare Conquest 2★ 300K/10min to 3★ 400K/12min: 1.8M vs 2.0M/hour excluding overhead; 3★ must be faster than **13m20s** to win on gross natural-claim Kina/hour at a 10-minute 2★ baseline.
- `2,100 → 2,500` adds **80K/claim**, equivalent to +1.68M/week on 21 natural claims or +3.28M/week on 41 eligible claims. Do not buy a 10M upgrade merely to gain that reward if you're seeking short-term Quna: payback is over 3 temporary shop-inclusive weeks before costs.
- For the **Spiritmaster (Global Summoner)**, maximize orb weapon/guard and cheap +10/Daevanion/skill improvements that actually improve reliable PvE clears; maintain Fire Spirit / Combustion / Elemental Fusion / Corrode and survival options appropriate to solo content. This is a build archetype, not a measured personal priority order. NCSOFT's October 14 patch fixed an Ancient Spirit activation issue below/equal level 15; old damage tests may no longer be comparable. Sources: https://aion2data.com/guides/spiritmaster ; https://lounge.plaync.com/feed/84134?country=US&locale=en-US ; https://aion2tierlist.com/guides/aion-2-spiritmaster-guide .
- **Transfers before expensive enhance:** Global community/client checks suggest +0→+10 Unique success is guaranteed; later +11→+15 is chance-based and item-level cost differs. Same-type Unique-to-Unique gear may carry enhancement and transferable Soul Binding, while stones/Potential and donor are lost. Before paying for +15 on expensive target gear, compare low-item-level Unique donor + Transfer Stone vs direct enhancement, using the actual in-client preview. Dated nonofficial model cites ~5.3M vs 12.6M expected expense for +15 on item level 36 vs 86, before donor and transfer-stone costs; **not a promise of net savings**: https://www.elitegrindlab.com/aion-2/enhancement-transfer/ and https://lum.gg/aion-2/guides/how-to-transfer-gear-enhancements .
- **Alts:** get the main into profitable sustainable content first. Alts on the same server can make 21 natural claims/week and transfer eligible tradeable Kina through Server Storage. Aim for roughly **1,400** on each before investing towards 2,100; account for leveling time, upgrade costs and shared weekly reductions. With the temporarily higher shop cap, **two characters × 41 claims = 82 claims**, near the 84-run Conquest full-pay limit only if one claim per run; with membership doubling claims per run, **82 claims take ~42 runs**, so check *runs*, not claims. Market/class assumptions and release rules remain unverified for each alt; avoid blindly replicating stale 25.5M/week claims.

## Operating checklist (no automatic execution)

1. **Before spending:** check the real character's level, item level, current Conquest/Transcendence `Weekly Cumulative Play Reward`, current bound vs tradeable Kina, subscription, server and market settlement. If not yet level 45, finish progression/quests to reach dungeon income before treating exchange arbitrage as viable.
2. **Daily (first 10–15 min):** do five server-shared Duty Missions on the main for bound upgrade funds; check Odyle cap and avoid wasted regen, and check current vendor price/limit. Do not spend premium Quna or buy currency from third-party sellers.
3. **Claims:** choose highest *net/hour* tier that consistently clears all bosses. With membership, use two reward cubes in a run when enough Odyle. Prefer 20 temporary vendor purchases **when the post-taper claim reward exceeds 100K plus variable cost**; route to the other mode if weekly payout reduction overwhelms the higher-tier reward.
4. **Gather or sell:** manually sample 20–30 minutes of Odyle/mats, estimate *realized* Market net/hour after fees; compare to your dungeon value. Prefer selling valuable materials over morphing when the material sale exceeds the energy's incremental cube value.
5. **Invest first:** reserve the next positive-payback combat upgrade, plus up to **2M `Kina (All)`** for this week's 20 vendor offers if eligible. Spend bound Kina first where possible; preserve tradeable Kina for Market items and eventual exchange.
6. **Convert surplus:** compare current 10M ask against 25×400K **settled** prices and actual fill times. Sell a 10M chest only if the price premium and liquidity justify tying up 11M tradeable Kina; use smaller boxes if you need Quna sooner for cosmetics. 2M is the cheapest **buyer** option in the October 19 screenshot, not the recommended seller denomination.
7. **Re-measure:** a new patch, server transfer, price collapse, unusual restrictions or gear-gate change invalidates older advice. Record anonymized timestamped Market receipts and clear-time averages; no live-feed/automation claims.
