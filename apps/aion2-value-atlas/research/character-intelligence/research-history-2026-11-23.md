## Mathematical contract

1. Preserve a canonical build B containing region, patch, class, item IDs/tiers, per-line stat IDs/units/values, stones, enhancements, boards, buffs, skill levels/specialties, and encounter assumptions. Reconcile reported aggregate stats with item contributions: use baseline reported effective stats plus recomputed deltas where valid, never sum the same gear twice. Unknown/hidden components remain unknown, not zero. Different buckets (Attack vs Attack Bonus vs conditional PvE Attack; crit rating vs crit chance; skill-specific bonuses) must not be conflated.
2. **Source invariance:** if two legal builds yield identical complete effective state (including buffs, skills, set effects, restrictions, conditional buckets and rotation), they must have identical modeled combat utility. Source slot has no standalone performance coefficient. Equal roll amounts can be interchangeable even when adoption differs. Different roll ranges, mutually exclusive alternatives, stat transformation order, source-specific effects and costs may change the optimum. CP invariance is unproven; test separately.
3. S(B)=normalize(base, all gear, progression, skills, buffs). Encounter c and skill j yield action frequency u_j(B,c), conditioned on cooldown, animation, resource budget, movement, uptime and chains. Expected damage is D(B,c)=sum_j u_j * sum_event p(event|j,S,c) * damage(j,event,S,c), with separate player and spirit actions. Events model crit/noncrit, clean hit/block/parry/evade, Double/Perfect/Multi-hit, positioning, phases and forced outcomes jointly where dependent. Include skill flat/scaled damage, attack composition, defense/penetration, damage boost/tolerance, conditional damage, crit multiplier and target state; avoid multiplying correlated outcomes as independent. Where data are incomplete, provide an explicitly conditional damage index/range, not fake exact DPS.
4. For a restricted illustrative model only, q=clamp((rating - targetResist)/1000,0,0.5) for a reported Global boss setting; crit multiplier E=1+q*b where b is effective bonus over noncrit. A line adds relative E'/E-1, not a fixed stat weight. The general engine must support competing curves and ordering; cap/rating conversion are patch/target-dependent. Forced crit/stagger and skill-specific crit/accuracy need separate events. For accuracy use a calibrated clean-hit probability and distinct damage loss for block/parry and evasion, not a universal hard cutoff. User-defined accuracy threshold is an optional constraint, not a proven mechanical breakpoint.
5. For current build B and legal replacement x at line l, marginal change = D(B[l<-x],c)-D(B,c). Rank by full recomputation, not a dot product of fixed stat weights. Also search coordinated changes; a one-step greedy move can miss the best pair or skill breakpoint. Example: equal +40 crit from earring/torso interchangeable when all other effective stats match. Example: boots +40 accuracy closes a hypothetical 40-point shortfall, freeing a more valuable alternative elsewhere. Never recommend illegal duplicate types or unattainable rolls.
6. Optimize over the legal discrete domain: choose item/slot/line assignments (or keep), legal roll values, item-specific line count/type exclusivity, skill options, locked lines, resources, minimum accuracy/defense/HP, CP/IL requirements and allowed changes. Search bounded item configurations then whole-build combinations; exact enumeration is the small-domain reference. Use branch-and-bound with admissible bounds only, Pareto pruning only on provably monotone dimensions, or beam search labelled approximate with coverage/time limit. Preserve distinct state needed by skill thresholds and conditional effects. Compare against exact brute-force on tiny cases; report infeasibility, optimality status/gap and assumptions. Separate single change, coordinated swap, theoretical best gear and budgeted attainable target.
7. CP: train a transparent delta model from consented, matched before/after official observations controlling region/patch, class, gear and buffs; test held-out character/patch groups, MAE/error intervals and interactions. Use actual server CP and display predicted CP only when validated, otherwise unavailable. Item level and CP distinct. Do not substitute popularity, DPS, or a guessed linear score. Include observed CP/DPS tradeoff frontier.
8. Cost: state s holds all existing lines, resources, available locks and materials; action a has source-verified cost vector and transition kernel P(s'|s,a). Conditional single-line probabilities and full-roll joint probabilities are distinct. If independent stationary retries with acceptance probability p and identical cost c are verified, E[cost]=c/p and P(success in n)=1-(1-p)^n; otherwise use finite-horizon DP / stochastic shortest path with a stop option. Evaluate risk of worse roll/destructive reset, resource opportunity costs, probability of attaining target within budget, and intermediate playable states. Track bound/unbound Kina and materials separately. No automated in-game actions or purchases.

### Mathematical optimizer contract v2
Let x(i,c) be binary selection of one legal configuration c per slot i, including keep. Sum_c x(i,c)=1. Contributions v(i,c) include actual units and separate stat buckets. Base state + sum x*v + dependent transformations yields final effective state; keep item identity/skill/sets/conditional signatures when these affect transformations. Define resource vector costs r, locked lines, allowable stats/rolls, per-item uniqueness, skill choices and encounter constraints. Costs for stochastic rerolls are not deterministic additive unless a verified approximation says so.

For positive independent multipliers, maximize log D=Σlog(M_k) as equivalent objective; marginal utility of additive amount δ in bucket M is exactly log(1+δ/M), not fixed popularity/weights. Equal δ favors the smaller eligible multiplier, but compare actual legal roll sizes and skill coefficients. For joint events compute probability-weighted damage directly, not forced factorization. Derivatives provide search ordering only; finite deltas and threshold crossings determine results.

Encounter scoring: use per-scenario D(B,e) / baseline D(B0,e) for comparable relative gains, with user-selected positive scenario weights. Optional worst-case gain and regret vs scenario-specific best. Explicitly separate boss/phase/rotation weights, survivability constraints, CP/IL gates, and objective choices. If uncertain mechanics reverse an ordering, show sensitivity and lower-confidence recommendation rather than fake precise BiS.

Solver comparison:
- Reference exhaustive enumeration over deliberately small legal domains: exact proof oracle.
- Per-item combinations: 24 choose 5=42,504 stat-type sets for a five-line boots pool if all types eligible and no repeats, BEFORE roll values, locked lines or tier differences; weapon 16 choose5=4,368. These are combinatorial counts for listed pools, not measured search runtimes. Canonicalize equivalent permutations, never materialize full Cartesian product of all items. In maximum-roll ideal-only mode keep max of same stat only if all objective/constraint monotonicity conditions hold and no cost penalty; actual attainable search retains current rolls and costs.
- Branch-and-bound: incumbent L from feasible complete build, optimistic U for partial branch. For monotone nonnegative effective stat domains with fixed compatible transformation signature, remaining coordinate maxima overestimate any joint feasible completion; evaluate optimistic monotone envelope, not a feasible candidate. For nonmonotone or uncertain mechanics use verified interval upper bounds or disable pruning. Prune only when U<=L (or objective-specific tolerance with reported gap), or constraint infeasibility proven by safe remaining bounds. At timeout report best incumbent and certified outstanding upper bound where available; gap=(U-L)/L for positive L. Without admissible bound label result heuristic, not proven optimum.
- Pareto DP: prune state dominated on all monotone performance dimensions and resource consumption only if identical relevant conditional/set/skill feasibility signatures and shared remaining decisions; skill-level thresholds and timing bins must be retained. Never merge states merely by equal scalar DPS when future combinations differ.
- CP-SAT / integer model ([official Google OR-Tools guide](https://developers.google.com/optimization/cp/cp_solver/)): excellent for legal binary selection and integer constraints. Nonlinear combat objective needs validated piecewise envelopes, integer scaling and bounded approximation, or candidate-generation followed by exact evaluator. CP-SAT's OPTIMAL means optimum of encoded model, not proof of true DPS or nonlinear approximation. Avoid bringing solver dependencies into static site before measuring native JS search feasibility.
- Specialized multidimensional knapsack ([OR-Tools reference](https://developers.google.com/optimization/pack/knapsack)): useful baseline for additive objectives/resource constraints; not sufficient for thresholded or interacting combat stats. Greedy/local search is cheap warm start and recommendation baseline, NOT optimum; beam search bounded approximate with diverse constraint-state bins. Benchmarks compare exact tiny domain, greedy baseline, branch-and-bound, Pareto and beam at equal work/time budgets; record nodes, wall time, memory, best valid score and certified gap, not invented performance.
- Cost planner: separate ideal theoretical target, budgeted legal build and ordered stochastic acquisition policy. Use Bellman recurrence V(s,b)=max{U(s),max_a ΣP(s'|s,a)V(s',b-c(a))} for finite budget/known costs and transitions; budget vector, current locks, legal state. Include stop option. If unavailable conditional distributions, no expected reroll price claim. Report chance to reach target in budget, quantiles and worst plausible costs when supported.

### 2. Architecture: demand-driven dependency graph (user-aware, not surveillance)
One source registry + resource cache + normalized semantic snapshot + dependency graph + presentation state. No blanket full refresh; no recurring collector or agent, no app activity when closed. On-demand user opens/resumes/selects page or character, presses refresh or reports change, changes objective/encounter/budget/hypothetical stats; these events resolve the transitive dependency closure of that view. Current user context is explicit local character selection, region/server, selected tabs, visible page/focus, target boss, goal, budget and locked lines. No process monitoring, account credentials, cross-site history, foreground capture, or install. Keep link and optional cached data local by default with clear Forget character/remove cached data control. Search queries transient, no public repo snapshots. If source requires account/API key, leave disabled until authorized; do not embed keys in Pages frontend, URLs, logs, Git.

Source registry minimum: sourceId, entityFamily, origin, base/path and query allowlist, region/locale, transport/permission state, schema version/validation, upstream build/patch, evidence status, HTTP validators/cache policy, provider quotas, last attempted/success/observed, error/retryAfter, capability status (available/unsupported/not verified/permission blocked/degraded), safe fallback and dependencies. Never dynamically enable discovered URL or arbitrary proxy. Respect HTTP cache directives (RFC9111 https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9111.html), conditional GET if ETag/Last-Modified supported, no-store and must-revalidate, 429 Retry-After, no retries on denied/private. Direct observed responses do not prove CORS; test deployed origin. If direct browser blocked, existing authorized same-origin broker only after verifying cost/permission; no new paid resource. Server-side shared cache only where appropriate permitted public data, bounded size, no cross-user leakage; private/local character data not global public cache.

Normalized entities split IMMUTABLE catalog definitions (region/build/id/version), MUTABLE character slot instances (characterKey/slot/itemId/observed roll/stone/skill/upgrade details), progression/board nodes (characterKey/boardId/node states), event/encounter/market observations and derived optimizer results. Drop irrelevant view counters, sort set-like collections only, preserve order where meaningful; normalize units explicitly. Semantic hash cannot infer absent/unexposed fields. A successful fetch is not proof upstream armory reflects last in-game action.

### Mathematical policy: from static optimizer to rolling planning
State s = observed character/skill/board/collections + patch + available inventory/resources + completion/recharge/lockouts + candidate catalog + time horizon + goals + uncertainty. Each field carries region, timestamp, known/unknown, observed vs hypothetical, source. Action a = prerequisites P(s), cost vector c(s,a) (currency, attempts, time, material, lost items, opportunity), transition kernel K(s'|s,a), deadline, scope (character/server/account), carryover/transfer and evidence. User explicitly executes recommended actions manually; system recalculates on semantic refresh/mutation signal using v5. Plan can choose wait, keep, claim, learn, farm, craft, exchange, equip, enhance, reroll or practice; no automatic game actions. Unknown prerequisite or cost => conditional recommendation/ask, not feasible by default.

Use Pareto frontier across effective combat performance, next-gate time, expected resource spend, risk, survivability, permanence, time and player-selected preference; hard constraints for budget, allowed change and safety. Avoid adding incomparable currencies or double-counting gate value and its downstream rewards. Conditional objective examples:
- next gate: minimize expected time/cost or maximize chance of reaching IL threshold by horizon, not simply IL/Kina; no false gate feasibility with incomplete prereqs.
- actual power: maximize encounter-weighted D(B,e), or robust minimum gain, with clear probability/uptime and defensive constraints; distinguish simulated vs measured and skill/party effects. CP predictive model still unavailable; use server-observed CP.
- progress per session: maximize expected downstream utility of end-state and accumulated useful rewards, with resource inventory and cap/deadline transitions; use finite-horizon planning / stochastic shortest path with replanning after each actual action, a stop/wait alternative and uncertainty. Exact small-domain oracle; practical candidate generator, dependency graph, dominance/branch bounds, rolling horizon/beam with honesty about gap. No need for a universal giant solver when independent subproblems can be decomposed safely; preserve shared resources and interactions.
- opportunity cost of item/material r = value of best foregone feasible use, even if already owned (not sunk acquisition price). Keep vector costs when exchange rates unknown, estimate marginal resource shadow values only from a validated constrained model; no invented market prices.
- gate option value: compare complete paths including downstream access/reward and risk. A small IL-only action may dominate expensive damage upgrade if it opens a repeatable superior source, but only if user can clear and will claim. Model reward probabilities, pity, lockout and time, not theoretical boss reward alone.
- recurring resource: if current energy e, cap C, refill r/hour, next opportunity h, overflow=max(0,e+r*h-C) under continuous hypothetical model; real discrete tick/reset/time zone needed. Choose spend enough to avoid overflow and retain reserve for higher-return content. No invented current counters; never assume every daily activity must be done.
- stochastic upgrades: compare success within budget, expected total cost, downside and stop rule. With state-dependent success p_i after i failures, expected attempts=sum_{k>=0} product_{j<k}(1-p_j) until guaranteed/bounded stop; per-step costs and destructive outcomes require full state transition. F3 expressly rules out naive stationary probability for conditional Sync/Bind. Track chance of any acceptable useful stat outcome, not only one popular line.
- value of information: if uncertain inputs can reverse the top recommendation, request smallest useful confirmation (one screen/field, not a long questionnaire). VOI concept E[max_a E(U|new observation)] - max_a E(U) minus observation cost, with uncertainty distributions only if justified. For low-value missing inputs proceed with robust/conditional option; if missing core profile, no personalized best path.
